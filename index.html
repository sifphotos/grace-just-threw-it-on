03 · wardrobe

the fit.

08

the fur is the fit. one statement fur, faux is perfect, worn open like a robe. keep what's under it simple and let the coat do the talking. i'm open on the exact pieces. the vibe matters more than the specifics.

wardrobe refs for the vibe only. not exact pieces, and not for hair.

the coatoversized, a big collar or a hood, long or cropped. cream, brown, leopard or black.
underneatha vibrant bra top, a set, a bodysuit, a simple top or low rise jeans. a pop of color under a neutral fur works.
legs + shoesbare or sheer or glossy tights. pointed heels, mules or knee boots.
extrassunglasses, a little bag, some jewelry. a phone always helps.

i can shop for the coat if you don't have one. fashion nova pieces are welcome if they fit the vibe, just send me what you're thinking.
